# Development protocol v1

Status: locally frozen before development learning; not an externally registered preregistration. Configuration A is `configs/development.json`. This document fixes decisions before inspecting its outcomes.

## Question and estimands

A is a trained Double-DQN checkpoint. I is the same checkpoint after whole-MLP output-preserving Plasticity Injection. D_A and D_I are the chronological transitions generated by their independently continued actors. The first cell letter is the learner initialization; the second is the tape's actor.

Primary conditional learner effects are `g_A = Y_IA - Y_AA` and `g_I = Y_II - Y_AI`, where Y is normalized discounted greedy-return AUC. A material sign reversal is the target phenomenon. Report all six raw cell curves, including fresh F on each tape. Fresh-network level AUC is descriptive, not an intrinsic-plasticity calibration.

Secondary bookkeeping:

- Total effect: `Y_II - Y_AA`.
- Learner-state allocation: `((Y_IA-Y_AA)+(Y_II-Y_AI))/2`.
- Generated-data allocation: `((Y_AI-Y_AA)+(Y_II-Y_IA))/2`.
- Interaction: `Y_II-Y_IA-Y_AI+Y_AA = g_I-g_A`.

The allocations add to the total algebraically. They are not unique natural mediation effects, and the algebra is not novelty. Never divide by a near-zero total to manufacture a large percentage.

## Environment and information boundary

Censored Branch has two actions, root state 0, two four-step branches, and terminal state 9. The root action chooses a branch. Each subsequent correct binary code bit advances; a wrong bit terminates with reward -0.05. Finishing a branch yields its context-specific reward. Episodes have at most five decisions.

Each structural seed generates fixed, unique 32-dimensional Rademacher observations and a separately seeded context schedule. The learner sees only the current embedding, action/reward/next embedding, and true termination. No context, oracle action, code, reward table, boundary flag, or diagnosis result enters its Q-network. The evaluator and simulator retain this privileged information. Context is hidden and changes over training; this is a nonstationary partially observed problem, not a stationary fully observed MDP claim.

Training context k has random codes and branch rewards (1.0,0.2) for even k and (0.2,1.0) for odd k. Context changes happen only at episode boundaries, after each scheduled 2,000-transition boundary. Aging stops at the first episode end at or after 80,000 interactions, so up to four extra transitions are counted. Whole aging blocks keep neural and tabular comparators in the same final context.

Post-checkpoint switches:

1. Visible refit: flip every bit of the old-best branch; keep rewards.
2. Hidden opportunity: leave the entire old-best path unchanged; increase the other branch reward to 1.5.
3. Mixed: flip the old-best code, reduce its reward to 0.2, and increase the other reward to 1.5.

An unchanged old path cannot reveal an unvisited improvement. That constructed fact alone does not pass the novelty gate.

## Learners and exact intervention

Neural learner: float64 NumPy Double DQN, 32→64→64→2 MLP, ReLU, mean Huber loss, Adam (0.9,0.999, epsilon 1e-8), learning rate 0.0003, discount 0.97, batch 32, one update per four incoming transitions, hard target copy every 250 updates. Online Q selects bootstrap actions; target Q evaluates them. True termination suppresses bootstrap. Target copying happens after an update. Action and replay RNG streams are separate; a random action draw is consumed every interaction independently of whether exploration is selected.

Replay capacity is **512**, not the 8,192 in the initial candidate sketch. This pre-data design correction avoids making most adaptation updates learn obsolete contexts. The full aged buffer is still shared by the four primary cells. The recent-replay comparator uses a rolling capacity of 128, not a one-time purge followed by regrowth.

Injection follows Plasticity Injection Equation 1: `old(x) + (g(x)-g0(x))`. Freeze the old function, initialize g, and freeze an identical g0. Only g is trainable; reset its Adam state. Preserve old online and old lagged-target functions separately at the intervention instant. Later target sync copies the online base and trainable network into their target counterparts. Parenthesized subtraction makes initial preservation exact in float64 for identical branches. Repeated injection is unsupported and rejected.

The trainable parameter count remains 6,402. Inference after injection evaluates three MLPs; frozen parameters and computation are not free. Record actual network/example forwards, their frozen subset, and evaluation forwards. This is the whole-network version of the published equation, not its shared-encoder Atari implementation. No pure-plasticity or equal-total-capacity claim follows.

The head-reset and identity transforms are executable secondary/sentinel configurations. The primary development intervention is injection only.

## Tapes and integrity

Actors alone choose training actions and touch the training simulator. Consumers append the same source transitions chronologically and use its exact absolute transition UIDs and target-sync schedule. UIDs identify chronological arrivals, never mutable ring slots. A consumer cannot access a future transition. Its bootstrap values remain its own: only data and update schedule are yoked.

A-on-D_A and I-on-D_I must reproduce the originating actor's entire learning fingerprint after **every update**, each scheduled evaluation, and the final state. Any mismatch invalidates the run; this is not a negative scientific result. Identity intervention must produce identical tapes and zero effects. Persist aged checkpoints, raw transitions, replay UID matrices, sync steps, actor fingerprints, curves, config bytes, and code/environment bindings. An exact deterministic sentinel must reproduce semantic outputs in a new run directory.

## Controls and clean-tape calibration

- Plain Double DQN is A.
- F is a fresh MLP trained on each primary tape; it starts from different predictions and is not the clean calibration.
- Oracle-boundary epsilon restart raises epsilon from 0.1 to 0.5 for the first 1,000 post-switch interactions. This intentionally privileged cheap exploration control is labeled as such.
- Recent-replay Double DQN keeps the checkpoint but uses a rolling 128-transition buffer.
- Independently aged tabular Q-learning uses the same observation information, a favorable lookup-table representation, learning rate 0.1, its own generated experience, and rolling 128-transition post-switch replay. It is not a neural plasticity measure. Its extra aging interactions are counted separately.
- A separate uniformly random behavior tape is generated with an initially empty buffer. A, I, and optimizer-reset-only O consume that same chronological tape. A and I start with identical online/target predictions; O changes only Adam moments and clocks. Clean gain is I-A AUC; excess gain is I-O AUC. This checks response to the intervention under common fresh support, not historical loss of intrinsic plasticity.

## Evaluation and units

After 0,250,...,5,000 post-switch interactions, evaluate the deterministic greedy policy from the root with discount 0.97 and lowest-action tie breaking. One rollout is exact in this deterministic family; repeating it 32 times would create no additional evidence. Dynamic programming supplies exact uniform-policy and **clairvoyant context-optimal** returns. Normalize `(J-J_uniform)/(J_clairvoyant-J_uniform)` without clipping; negative scores are legitimate. Integrate the normalized curve by trapezoids over the fixed horizon. This measures discounted greedy control adaptation, not lifetime exploratory return or deployment safety.

Development: structural seeds 101,202,303 crossed with learner seeds 11,22. Average learner initializations within each structural seed, then summarize three structural clusters. Deterministic percentile bootstrap intervals resample structural clusters only and are explicitly exploratory with so few clusters. Do not count evaluations, minibatches, switches, or the six correlated pairs as six independent environment samples.

Training and post-switch evaluation occur inside each procedural development world. No evaluation interaction is inserted into replay. Locked testing uses six new structural seeds and three new learner seeds, derived from `seed_for("yoked-locked-structure-v1",i)` and `seed_for("yoked-locked-learner-v1",i)` only after a passing development decision. The CLI requires a hash-verified passing development prerequisite, matching core settings, and disjoint seeds. Held-out worlds are not to be run after a failed development gate.

## Frozen preliminary advance gates

All three are required, on structural-cluster means:

1. Clean calibration: at least one visible/mixed switch has I-A >= 0.10 and I-O >= 0.05 on the fresh uniform tape.
2. Hidden data: hidden-opportunity total effect >= 0.05, generated-data allocation >= 0.05, and data allocation >= one third of total.
3. Novelty: at least one switch has `g_A*g_I < 0`, `min(abs(g_A),abs(g_I)) >= 0.05`, and `abs(g_I-g_A) >= 0.15`.

A point-estimate pass is only permission for a locked test. It is not confirmation, especially with three development clusters. A paper-level result additionally needs held-out intervals supporting both opposing conditional signs, robustness to replay/optimizer/exploration controls, and a renewed closest-work audit. The initial CLI's gate is deliberately a *preliminary* gate even in a held-out summary; do not call that summary a confirmatory decision by itself.

## Bounded adjustment and stop policy

No outcomes were inspected before this protocol/configuration freeze. There is at most one exact rerun for a diagnosed transient/integrity problem, in a new immutable run directory. A scientific failure is not transient.

One possible cause-directed development adjustment is reserved: if the clean calibration fails, change only the learning rate from 0.0003 to 0.001 and keep every seed, threshold, architecture, environment, and budget fixed. The hypothesis is that low-rate aging did not instantiate a material intervention response; the higher rate is motivated by the published injection sensitivity analysis. Record the failure and the cause before running configuration B. Do not make that adjustment merely to rescue a failed sign-reversal or hidden-data gate when clean calibration already passes. No third configuration, threshold relaxation, hidden seed inspection, or environment search is allowed in this launch.

If the permitted branch still fails, stop the positive thesis and preserve the negative result. Further work would require a new explicitly justified protocol, not silent continuation of this one.
